# Demo script (4 minutes)

| Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Dashboard, map of Sikkim villages, all green/low | "This is TeestaGuard — a last-mile early warning system for Sikkim's Teesta valley, built after the October 2023 South Lhonak glacial lake outburst flood killed 46 people and destroyed the Teesta III dam." |
| 0:20–0:40 | Click Chungthang village | "Each village gets a live risk score, built from rain, river level, glacial lake exposure and landslide risk — all explained in plain language, not a black box." |
| 0:40–1:10 | Open Demo controls → Simulate surge | "Let's simulate what the state's gauges would have reported that night — a surge at the Chungthang gauge." Watch the map turn red downstream, village by village. |
| 1:10–1:30 | Chungthang auto-selected, Severe, reasons shown | "Chungthang and Toong flip to Severe immediately, Dikchu to High — and the system tells the officer exactly why." |
| 1:30–2:00 | Alerts tab → New alert → composer | "One click opens an alert for every High or Severe village, previewed in English, Hindi and Nepali before it goes out." Switch to the नेपाली tab. |
| 2:00–2:15 | Send alert, toast confirms | "Sent — to every affected village, in three languages, in one action." |
| 2:15–2:45 | Switch to /citizen, choose Toong, language Nepali | "On the resident's side: their own risk, in their own language, and the alert banner they'd actually see." |
| 2:45–3:00 | Tap "I'm safe" | "A one-tap check-in." |
| 3:00–3:15 | Back on dashboard, Activity tab | "And it shows up on the officer's dashboard within seconds." |
| 3:15–3:40 | Citizen app: report an incident with a photo | "Residents can also report flooding or a blocked road, with a photo — which appears instantly in the officer's activity feed." |
| 3:40–4:00 | Dashboard, Reset demo | "Everything here runs offline with bundled sample data, so the demo never breaks — and it's reset with one click." |
